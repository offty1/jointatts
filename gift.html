Good to know
What can it be used for?

Any custom black and grey tattoo by Daria: the design and the session. It can also cover the booking deposit. Daria works in black and grey realism only (no color, no fine line).

How does the recipient book?

They send a request through the booking form or DM @join.tatts and mention the certificate. The design is drawn for them and shared before the session.

What if the tattoo costs more or less?

If the tattoo costs more, they simply pay the difference. If it costs less, the remaining balance stays on the certificate for next time.

Does it expire?

No, the certificate doesn’t expire.

Age

The person getting tattooed must be 18 or older, with a valid photo ID at the appointment.
