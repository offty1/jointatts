Privacy Policy

This page explains how I handle the information you share through the tattoo request form on this site. I’m Daria, a tattoo artist in Seattle, and I’m the only person who reads your request. This site has no ads, tracking pixels or analytics.

What I collect

Your name, how you’d like to be contacted (Instagram, email or phone), the details of your tattoo idea (description, placement, size, timing, budget and any notes) and any reference photos you upload, including photos of existing tattoos or scars.

How I use it

Only to understand your idea, draw your design, give you an estimate and book your appointment. I never sell your information or use it for marketing.

Your photos

Reference images and photos of your skin are used only to plan your tattoo. I never post anything about your tattoo online without asking you first.

Where it’s stored

Requests are saved to my private Google account (Drive, Sheets and email). Google stores this data on my behalf. No one else has access.

Text messages

If you choose Text, I’ll only message you about your request and appointment. Reply STOP anytime to opt out.

How long I keep it

As long as needed for booking, touch-ups and my records. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing book@jointatts.com or messaging @join.tatts on Instagram.

Questions

Write to book@jointatts.com and I’ll get back to you.
